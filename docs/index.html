Telugu Panchangam

Subscribe to a Telugu Panchangam calendar feed. Every day shows Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, auspicious and inauspicious timings, and special day markers — directly in your calendar.

1. Pick your city and calculation system

Drik Ganita
Modern observational astronomy. Most accurate for actual sky events (sunrise, moonrise). Used by popular digital Panchangam apps.
Surya Siddhanta
Ancient classical system. Used by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams and most temple rituals.
Vakya
Traditional South Indian system using pre-computed correction tables. Widely followed by Telugu and Tamil printed Panchangams.

3. Subscribe in your calendar app

  • Google Calendar: Open Google Calendar → Other calendars (+ icon) → From URL → paste the URL → Add calendar
  • Apple Calendar: File → New Calendar Subscription → paste URL → Subscribe
  • Outlook: Add Calendar → From Internet → paste URL

Your calendar will refresh automatically each month with updated Panchangam data.

What's in each day's event

Each day appears as an all-day banner event (no calendar blocking). Tap any day to see:

  • Samvatsara, Maasam, Paksham, Vaaram
  • Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana — with start/end times
  • Sunrise, Sunset, Moonrise, Moonset
  • Auspicious: Brahma Muhurta, Abhijit Muhurta, Amrita Kalam
  • Inauspicious: Rahu Kalam, Gulika Kalam, Yamagandam, Varjyam, Durmuhurtham
  • Choghadiya blocks

Special days (Ekadashi, Amavasya, Pournami, Pradosham, Sankranti) are marked with ⚡ in the title so they stand out at a glance.
