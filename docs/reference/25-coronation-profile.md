# Coronation Source Profile

## Claim and authority

The `coronation` activity is linked to `muhurta.coronation`. Its authority is
B. V. Raman's *Muhurtha*, Chapter XVI, "Electing Head of the State" and
"Coronation," internal printed page 66 (physical PDF page 70 in the inspected
2020 Chistabo derivative). Raman
is a modern secondary authority, not scripture.

The coronation paragraph says the immediately preceding head-of-state rules
apply. The profile therefore admits the passage's twelve Nakshatras, its
specified Tithis, and its eight rising Rasis. The text admits odd Shukla
Tithis except Navami, plus Dwitiya and Dashami; the latter two are represented
in both Pakshas because the passage does not qualify them by Paksha.

## Chart-level conditions

The source also requires strength for Surya, Chandra, Lagna, the 10th house
and their lords, with a vacant 8th and malefics confined to Upachayas. These
remain disclosed manual checks because the finder does not evaluate all of
those chart relationships.

Raman then distinguishes two preferred configurations: Simha rising with
Surya there and Guru's aspect for a traditional coronation; or a
Kumbha-rising Shani configuration for the beginning of democratic rule. They
are alternatives, not universal hard gates, and remain manual checks.
