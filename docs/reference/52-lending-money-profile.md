# Lending-money source profile

## Authority and role

The `lending_money` activity follows B. V. Raman, *Muhurtha (Electional
Astrology)*, Chapter X, “Lending Money,” printed page 45 (PDF page 49). It is
the lender or creditor side of making a loan. Borrowing, receiving repayment
and investing capital are separate activities.

Raman says the otherwise prohibited stars are suitable for receiving money.
That observation is documented but is not exposed as a repayment-receipt
profile without its own complete crosswalk.

## Exact automated gates

- Reject Krittika, Magha, Moola, Shatabhisha, Uttara Phalguni and Punarvasu.
  Raman's abbreviated “Uttara” is resolved as Uttara Phalguni, consistent with
  his naming convention elsewhere in the edition.
- Reject the lender's Janma Nakshatra when supplied.
- Reject Tuesday and Friday; admit the other five weekdays without inventing
  preference bonuses.
- Reject Amavasya only when the weekday is Saturday. Neither all Saturdays nor
  every Amavasya is prohibited by this sentence.

## Chart checks and independent-practice conflict

The Lagna and 7th lords should be harmonious, and Chandra in Vrischika is
described as adverse for the lender. These remain manual prerequisites, so an
automated result cannot receive `Excellent`.

Drik Panchang's current loan-giving methodology instead says not to lend on
Wednesday. That directly differs from Raman, who excludes Tuesday and Friday
and therefore admits Wednesday. The published-practice disagreement is stored
as `muhurta.lending.drkpanchang_divergence`; the product follows its explicitly
named Raman lineage rather than silently combining both.

## Financial safety

Timing cannot establish creditworthiness, affordability, enforceability,
repayment probability or investment safety. Credit assessment, documentation,
interest and collateral law, concentration risk and qualified advice govern
the decision.
