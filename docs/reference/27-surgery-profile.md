# Surgery Source Profile

## Safety boundary

Muhurtam is never a reason to postpone urgent, necessary, or
clinician-directed medical care. The treating clinician's instructions and
the patient's medical needs always take priority. This boundary is published
with the activity's manual checks, not left only in documentation.

## Claim and authority

The `surgery` activity is linked to `muhurta.surgery`. Its electional
authority is B. V. Raman's *Muhurtha*, Chapter XV, "Surgical Operations,"
printed pages 64-65 (PDF pages 68-69 in the registered edition). Raman is a
modern secondary authority, not scripture or medical authority.

The passage recommends Tuesday or Saturday during Chandra's increase, rejects
the exact full Moon, and names Chaturthi, Navami or Chaturdashi with Ardra,
Jyeshtha, Ashlesha or Moola. The profile conservatively represents the waxing
condition as the three corresponding Shukla Tithis.

## Chart-level conditions

The source also says to avoid Chandra in the patient's natal Rasi and the Rasi
governing the operated body part, particularly under malefic affliction. It
calls for strong Mangala and a strong relevant house, a vacant 8th house, and
no mutual Mangala-Shani aspect. These remain visible practitioner checks
because the finder lacks the required patient and complete chart context.

The existing Vishti-Karana rejection has separate project provenance and does
not inherit authority from this passage. Python and MCP publish the stable
claim ID, exact automated gates, safety boundary, and chart checks.
