## Scope finding

The passage governs Shantika rites intended to pacify an affliction and
Paushtika rites intended to promote welfare. Its examples include auspicious
Puja and Mula-shanti, but it is not a universal election for every ceremony or
Puja. The commentary also makes an important pastoral exception: when an
ominous event itself creates the need for Shanti, ordinary timing defects need
not prevent the remedial rite.
