Muhurta day diagnosis, slot ranking, and chart screening ​

personal.muhurta-slot-ranking

Hard exclusions, time-window subtraction, personal strengths, textual preferences, disclosed heuristics, and a bounded source-backed election-chart post-screen produce ranked slots.

Computation method ​

Method type: algorithm

Apply profile day gates, remove candidate named Muhurtas that intersect hard windows, evaluate slot-time and source-specific personal facts, rank survivors with transparent additive scores and relative tiers, then use a privacy-minimal candidate-time chart service to apply only deterministic source predicates.

Process ​

  1. Validate activity and Chandra mode, load its profile, and return a diagnosis when a day-level gate fails.
  2. Build fifteen named day slots or fifteen night slots and remove those overlapping applicable hard windows.
  3. For each survivor choose dominant Choghadiya, recompute slot facts when an engine is supplied, and apply profile admission gates.
  4. Preserve all generic group scoring. For Drik, evaluate the named primary-traveller, householder, mother or patient rule from exact returned Chandra facts and the canonical local Lagna at every sampled state; reject a prohibition present at any sample and count a preference only when it persists across all samples.
  5. Add disclosed contributions from Muhurta nature, Choghadiya, Tara, Chandra, Tithi, Vara, special/Nitya/Anandadi Yogas, Amrita, Lagna, Hora, Panchaka, and profile preferences or penalties.
  6. Attach grouped reasons, personal/day dosha flags, and manual rows classified by the generated structured contract for all 30 browser activities, then assign relative tiers and form the base-ranked candidate list.
  7. For a Drik activity with deterministic chart rules, sample each window's start, final represented minute and 10-minute cadence plus the minute before, at, and after every known interior Drik Lagna transition; map each sampled minute to the validated local Lagna. If boundary support is missing, retain and cap the base shortlist as unavailable.
  8. Dynamically pack no more than 24 ordered UTC instants into each request, validate the complete nine-graha Lahiri response, ignore sidecar house numbers, and recompute every Whole Sign house from planetary Rashi relative to the canonical local Lagna before evaluating generated predicates.
  9. If a window only partly overlaps the five-minute transition guard, hold every Lagna-dependent general predicate and Travel/Gruhapravesha Lagna rule as unknown; retain and cap the window for review without inventing a rejection or preference. Continue Moon/Nakshatra-only Seemantha and Surgery personal rules.
  10. Apply personal removals first and chart removals second so the categories are exclusive, then refill from further base-ranked candidates when failures leave fewer than ten competitive survivors, bounded to five requests; if that cap is reached, report the exact screened-candidate count, while a service failure retains the original top ten with an unavailable state and an empty base list makes no request.
  11. Sort by tier, raw score, source-specific personal preferences, chart preferences, absence of a computed personal dosha, date and time; never present unresolved qualitative checks as computed.

Formulae ​

Relative tier coordinate ​

text
q = (score-floor)/(ceiling-floor); Excellent if q>=0.75, Good if q>=0.50, Fair if q>=0.25, else Avoid
  • floor, ceiling: minimum and maximum survivor scores in the batch

Inclusive personal position ​

text
position = ((target_index - natal_index + cycle_length) mod cycle_length) + 1
  • cycle_length: 12 for Rasi or 27 for Nakshatra
  • natal_index: primary participant's Janma Rasi/Lagna or birth-Nakshatra index
  • target_index: candidate Lagna or Nakshatra index

Canonical Whole Sign house ​

text
house(planet) = ((rashi_index(planet) - rashi_index(local_lagna) + 12) mod 12) + 1
  • rashi_index(planet): DashaFlow Lahiri planetary Rashi index 0-11
  • rashi_index(local_lagna): selected-city Lagna index 0-11 from the validated local Drik/Lahiri transition map
  • sidecar-returned house and Lagna fields are diagnostic and do not override this projection

Sampled election window ​

text
samples = unique([start_minute, max(start_minute, end_minute - 1)] + [start_minute + 10k while before final_minute] + [transition_minute - 1, transition_minute, transition_minute + 1 for each known interior Drik Lagna transition])
  • start_minute: first included civil minute
  • end_minute: exclusive civil-minute boundary
  • k: positive integer cadence step
  • transition_minute: precomputed Drik Lagna transition; transition samples are clipped to the half-open interval

Partial transition guard ​

text
review = window intersects [transition-5, transition+5] without fully spanning that guard
  • a review-gated general or Lagna-dependent personal rule resolves to unknown
  • a fully spanned guard evaluates both canonical local Lagna states

Worked example ​

Generic slot, Hyderabad 2026-06-11 ​

Inputs

  • Activity: any
  • Slot: 07:26:23-08:18:59 IST
  • Mitra named Muhurta
  • Rog Choghadiya

Calculation

  1. Mitra auspicious nature +1; Rog Choghadiya +0.
  2. Amrita Kalam overlap +2, Sarvartha Siddhi +2, Shobhana +1, Anandadi Chhatra +1.
  3. Total score = 7; no personal or day dosha.
  4. This score is at the top relative band among surviving slots.

Result

  • Score: 7
  • Tier: Excellent

Wedding chart rejection at the sampled window edge ​

Inputs

  • Activity: wedding
  • Start snapshot projects Kuja to house 7 in the local Lagna frame
  • Final represented minute projects Kuja to house 8 in the local Lagna frame
  • Both snapshots contain all nine canonical grahas

Calculation

  1. The start snapshot passes wedding.kuja-not-8.
  2. The final snapshot fails wedding.kuja-not-8.
  3. A reject predicate fails when either sample contains the prohibited condition.
  4. The different statuses also mark the sampled window unstable.

Result

  • The candidate window is removed
  • No raw-score adjustment or re-tiering can override the source rejection

Method notes ​

  • The additive weights and relative-tier bands are project heuristics even when an individual activity gate has verified textual support.
  • Chart predicates are generated from the Python rule table and retain claim-specific source locators. The DashaFlow sidecar supplies Lahiri planetary Rashi/degree facts but does not choose an activity or issue an auspiciousness verdict; the browser's validated local Drik/Lahiri Lagna map owns the house frame.
  • Role preferences persist only stable saved-profile IDs in browser-local storage and are never sent, shared or exported; one-off participants remain session-only.
  • Edge, cadence and known Lagna-transition sampling improves whole-window coverage but does not establish continuous validity inside the entire interval.

Contract ​

Owning layer: personal
Claim kind: project-heuristic
Time basis: Day and night candidate windows use per-slot Panchangam facts. Source-specific personal and deterministic chart predicates sample each half-open window at its start, final represented civil minute, every 10 civil minutes from the start, and the minute before, at, and after every known interior Drik/Lahiri Lagna transition. Each sample minute is mapped to its canonical local Lagna before Whole Sign houses are projected.

Inputs ​

  • Panchangam days
  • activity profile
  • birth profiles and an activity-specific primary role
  • travel direction
  • slot duration
  • candidate city coordinates and IANA timezone
  • candidate-time DashaFlow Lahiri planetary positions
  • validated selected-city Drik/Lahiri Lagna transitions

Outputs ​

  • day diagnosis
  • candidate slots
  • scores
  • relative tiers
  • reasons
  • manual checks
  • personal-role and chart-screening states
  • per-rule pass/fail/unknown outcomes
  • removed and retained chart-screened slots

Limitations ​

  • Relative rankings mean best available under the inputs, not absolute scriptural quality or professional advice.
  • Automatic integration of election-chart enrichment and the four source-specific personal-role screens into ranked output is website-only and Drik/Lahiri/Whole Sign only; pure Python evaluators provide parity, but Python/MCP slot ranking does not apply them or call the DashaFlow chart service, and Surya Siddhanta or Vakya results are not blended with them.
  • Start/end, 10-minute cadence, and every known interior Drik Lagna transition are sampled, but this remains a discrete check rather than a continuous-time proof for every instant inside a window; missing Lagna boundary support makes screening unavailable.
  • DashaFlow and Drik Panchang can place an Ascendant transition one or two civil minutes apart. A slot that only partly overlaps the five-minute guard around a local transition keeps every Lagna-dependent general predicate and the Travel/Gruhapravesha Lagna rules unknown and review-gated; it is not automatically rejected or preferred. Seemantha and Surgery Moon/Nakshatra personal rules remain computable.
  • One search dynamically packs at most 24 unique instants per request and makes at most five chart requests; the number of highest-ranked base candidates screened is therefore data-dependent, and candidateLimitReached explicitly marks a short or empty result that did not exhaust every lower-ranked base candidate.
  • Positive chart and personal source preferences are tie-break evidence only and do not change the raw heuristic score.
  • Qualitative aspect, strength, dignity, benefic/malefic, lordship, Navamsa, compatibility, lineage and real-world safety judgments remain manual.
