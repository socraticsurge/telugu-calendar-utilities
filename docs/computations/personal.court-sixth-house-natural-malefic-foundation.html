Court sixth-house natural-malefic exclusion foundation ​

personal.court-sixth-house-natural-malefic-foundation

A Court-scoped reject predicate consumes the shared natural-graha classifier and requires every represented Whole Sign sixth-house state to remain free of resolved natural malefics.

Computation method ​

Method type: algorithm

Delegate natural-graha classification to the canonical shared decision table, inspect only Whole Sign house 6, and aggregate all represented states with reject-first precedence.

Process ​

  1. Reject an unavailable or conflicting local-Lagna house frame to unknown.
  2. Validate the complete canonical nine-graha chart through the shared natural-graha classifier.
  3. Fail when any resolved natural malefic occupies house 6, including fixed malefics, waning Chandra, or malefic-associated Budha.
  4. Return unknown when an occupant's Chandra or Budha nature remains conditionally unresolved and no resolved malefic already controls; otherwise pass.
  5. Project a known failure to reject, a pass to retain, and an unknown to review without yet wiring candidate generation.
  6. Across a window, any known failure controls; otherwise unknown controls; an all-pass result still becomes unknown when relevant transitions are incomplete or the request budget is exhausted.

Formulae ​

Court sixth-house exclusion ​

text
pass iff not exists p: H(p) = 6 and natural_nature(p) = malefic
  • H(p): canonical Whole Sign house from the validated local Drik/Lahiri Lagna
  • natural_nature(p): shared fixed, guarded-Chandra and conditional-Budha classification
  • unknown: used when a potentially decisive conditional nature or transition is unresolved

Worked example ​

Kuja in the sixth with an unrelated phase-unknown occupant ​

Inputs

  • Kuja occupies Whole Sign house 6
  • Chandra is inside the disclosed phase-boundary guard
  • All nine canonical graha facts are otherwise available

Calculation

  1. The shared classifier resolves Kuja as a fixed natural malefic.
  2. Kuja supplies a decisive forbidden house-6 witness.
  3. Reject-first precedence prevents the unrelated unknown state from hiding the known violation.

Result

  • Status: fail
  • Accepted Court effect: reject
  • No score or tier adjustment can override the exclusion

Method notes ​

  • The shared JSON oracle is synthetic contract evidence, not a source golden or external ephemeris validation.
  • The rule consumes personal.natural-graha-nature and does not create a second event-specific benefic or malefic taxonomy.
  • general_baseline_mode remains none, so the predicate is not double-counted as a future general election baseline check.

  • validated local-Lagna house-frame state

  • local-Lagna, graha-Rasi, Chandra-phase and Budha-association transition coverage

  • unwired reject, retain or review disposition under the accepted Court effect policy
